Task 7.23 corpus v1.0.0

All newly authored procedural raster and text corpus artifacts generated by
corpus.py for this task are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .

Construction uses self-authored quips and drawn shapes, deterministic random
textures, and Pillow's installed default font, with no downloaded or private
media. The manifest records the construction, provenance and original hash of
each artifact. This corpus is synthetic feature/size coverage, not production
traffic. Encoder dependencies and existing public protocol fixture keys,
evidence, source and history retain their existing respective licenses/terms;
this dedication does not relicense preexisting material.
