# ZoidbergChain Beta Tester Guide

## What is ZoidbergChain?

ZoidbergChain is an experimental meme-focused blockchain where the community reviews originality. Testers can submit content, vote on whether it looks original, and watch approved content move toward certified meme blocks.

## What is this beta testing?

This beta is a small controlled rollout for testing:

- access requests and invite codes
- MetaMask connection and wallet binding
- returning approved-wallet login
- content submission
- originality voting
- test ZOID rewards
- in-app feedback and operator support

## Important disclaimers

- ZoidbergChain is in controlled beta.
- Test ZOID has no real monetary value.
- Content, votes, accounts, and balances may be reset during testing.
- Wallets are used for identity and signatures.
- Never enter or share seed phrases, private keys, or passwords.
- Do not upload illegal, abusive, private, or copyrighted content you do not have permission to use.
- This is experimental software.

## What you need before starting

- MetaMask on desktop, or MetaMask Mobile on mobile
- a wallet you can reconnect with later
- a stable internet connection
- time to test both the happy path and anything confusing you notice

## How to request access

1. Open [zoidbergcoin.com](https://zoidbergcoin.com/).
2. On the access gate, choose `Request Beta Access`.
3. Fill in your name, email, and why you want access.
4. Wait for operator approval.

## How to use an invite code

1. Once approved, you will receive a one-time invite code from the team.
2. Return to the access gate and choose `I Have an Invite`.
3. Enter the invite code.
4. Continue into wallet connection and verification.

Invite codes are one-time and should not be shared.

## How to connect MetaMask

1. Press the MetaMask connect button when prompted.
2. Choose the wallet account you want to use for this beta.
3. Approve the connection in MetaMask.

If MetaMask is not detected on mobile, open the site inside the MetaMask Mobile browser and try again.

## How to bind your wallet

1. After your invite is accepted, connect MetaMask.
2. Sign the verification message.
3. Finish the `Bind Verified Wallet` step.

This binds your approved wallet to your access account so you can return later without reusing the invite code.

## How to reconnect as a returning user

1. Return to the site later.
2. Choose the returning-wallet path if needed.
3. Connect the same approved wallet.
4. Sign a fresh verification message.

If you switch to a different wallet, access does not automatically follow.

## How to submit a meme or other content

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Upload an image or enter text content.
3. Add a title or caption if needed.
4. Submit using your verified approved wallet.

Submission still requires normal beta access plus a verified wallet.

## How to vote ORIGINAL / NOT ORIGINAL / UNSURE

- Vote `ORIGINAL` when the content appears original and acceptable.
- Vote `NOT ORIGINAL` when it looks copied, stolen, or clearly derivative without permission.
- Vote `UNSURE` when you cannot confidently decide.

Vote carefully and do not vote on your own submission.

## How rewards and test ZOID work

- Test ZOID is beta-only and has no real monetary value.
- Rewards can depend on final outcomes and current eligibility rules.
- Balances and rewards may reset during testing.

## How to send feedback

- Use the in-app `Send Feedback` button from the access gate or dashboard.
- Include what page you were on, what device you used, and what you expected to happen.
- Do not include private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or invite codes.

## What to do if something breaks

1. Refresh the page.
2. Reconnect the same approved wallet.
3. Retry the wallet signature step if it was interrupted.
4. On mobile, reopen the site in MetaMask Mobile.
5. Send feedback from inside the app if the problem continues.

## Mobile instructions

- For best results, open the site in the MetaMask Mobile browser.
- If using Safari or Chrome and the wallet is not detected, open the site in MetaMask Mobile instead.
- App switching and wallet signing can vary by device and browser.
- Use the in-app feedback form if mobile wallet connection fails.

## Things not to share

Never share:

- seed phrases
- private keys
- passwords
- one-time invite codes

## Known beta limitations

- The beta is intentionally small and some actions remain approval-gated.
- Explorer and advanced activity views are still more technical than the main app.
- Content, votes, balances, and rewards may reset during testing.
- Mobile signing behavior can vary across browsers and devices.
